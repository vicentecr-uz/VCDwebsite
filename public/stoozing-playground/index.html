Display

Currency is used for labels, tiles, and chart amounts only. The underlying model stays the same numeric values.

Names (tiles & chart)

These labels appear on card tiles and in the chart legend. They do not change how the maths works.

Extra cards (credit limit only)

Each entry adds a tile and increases the denominator for overall credit utilisation %. Tiles always show a zero balance — extra cards are not part of the stooze simulation (they serve only as utilisation context). Full multi-card stooze is not simulated here.
